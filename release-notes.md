## What's new

- **Tokens always on top.** Spells are now drawn inside Atlas's own map: tokens, HP bars, nameplates, rotate and resize handles all stay on top at any zoom. The players' screen gets spells straight from Atlas's map too, under its fog of war.
- **Spells stay visible in the dark.** They're drawn above Atlas's scene lighting, so Darkness, and any spell in a dim room, shows at full strength.
- **Magical darkness blocks sight.** Darkness puts up a ring of sight-blocking Atlas walls round its edge: no one sees into, out of or through it, and light doesn't pass through. Tokens can still walk in. The walls move with it and go when it ends.
- **A spell's light fills its area.** A lit sphere is bright to its edge; a lit cone is bright right to its far corners, at the cone's own angle.
- **Ranges set in Atlas's light editor stay**, along with everything else set there.
- **More statblock spells are found:** spells named inside an action or reaction's text (Dispel Magic, Counterspell, Fireball in "Evocation Barrage"…), not only in spell lists.
- Behind the scenes: typed throughout, so Obsidian's review has (almost) nothing left to warn about.
