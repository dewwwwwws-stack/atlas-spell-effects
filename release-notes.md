## What's new

- **"On a creature" in the Area row.** Shield, Bless, Hold Person and the like play on the creature, round its token and over it, so they're always seen (before, an effect the size of a token hid under it). Choose **The caster** or **Each creature you click**. A spell with a range of Self and no area (Shield) is on the caster: from a statblock it lands at once, otherwise one click on the caster.
- **Deleting a saved spell asks "Are you sure?" in a pop-up**, and nothing moves around.
- **A tidier cast window, more like Atlas's own:** the star (✨ menu) and the bin (Delete) sit at the top beside the name; Area, Light and Animation are set apart by dividers; Snap to grid is with the area; the buttons fit on one row.
- **"Spell AoE player preview"** is the new name for showing players where you're aiming (and *Player preview style* for how it looks).
- **The settings name your game system** as Atlas has it ("D&D 5e", "Daggerheart", or your own).
