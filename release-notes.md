# Combat+ 1.13.13

- **Abilities that move the creature get a Use button.** "The gnoll moves up to half its Speed, and it makes one Rend attack", "the wolf moves up to 30 feet", "the spirit magically teleports up to 30 feet", Pathfinder's "Strides twice", Draw Steel's "shifts up to 2 squares", Daggerheart's "moves anywhere within Far range". How far it can go shows round the creature (half its Speed reads its statblock's Speed, and its flying speed for "fly up to half its flying speed"). Click where it goes and its token moves there; Ctrl+Z puts it back, Esc skips the move.
- **Whatever else the ability does follows in its order:**
  - an attack after the move or teleport ("…and it makes one Rend attack", "then makes two Tusk attacks") starts as soon as it lands: click who it hits;
  - an area or a save after the move (a shock wave "at the end of this movement", "each creature whose space it enters") is placed from where it lands;
  - an area or a save before it (a dragon's Wing Attack, "…can then fly up to half its flying speed") is placed first, then the move.
- A pushed target, an ally moving, or something the creature makes ("the ooze moves 10 feet") isn't read as the creature's own move.
- **A tidier cast window.** Settings are grouped into cards: Area and Look are always open; Movement, Light, Conditions, Also and Atlas area fold away and show what each is set to, so you can see everything at a glance and open only what you need. The colour sits with the animation, the animation list no longer takes over the window, and a line at the top sums up what will be placed. For an ability that only moves the creature, Movement comes first. Movement can also be set by hand: how far (half, its or twice its Speed, or a set distance) and whether it moves before or after the rest.
