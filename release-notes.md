## New

- **Animations for attacks (optional).** Turn on *Animations for attacks* (Settings → On the map) and plain attacks play an animation from the attacker to the target, picked by the weapon's name: a greatsword's swing, a longbow's arrow, a crossbow bolt, a thrown rock, a bite or claw strike, a punch. Magical attacks, like a mage's Arcane Burst, get the bolt of their damage type (Force, Fire…). Attacks with no named weapon use a default you choose for melee and for ranged, and any attack's animation can be changed and saved in its cast window. Every attack in the SRD was checked: all but 7 get their own weapon's animation.
- **What a save does besides damage** is shown on the card. A gold dragon's Weakening Breath, a medusa's gaze or Hold Person shows "Failed: …" (and "Saved: …" where it says) in the ability's own words. **Give** buttons still appear where a condition is named.
- **Atlas Online view.** A new switch at the top of the settings shows only what online players see: Atlas's own areas (with their Fire, Frost… looks), lights, conditions, terrain and summoned tokens. Animations and drawn shapes are hidden on your screen and the player window, since they never reach Atlas Online. While it's on, spells are made as Atlas areas. You still see where you're aiming. It has a hotkey command too.
  - **More shows up online:** in this view, spells on a creature (Bless, Hold Person) get an Atlas circle that follows it, bolts (Fire Bolt) get an Atlas line from caster to target, and walls (Wall of Fire) get an Atlas line along them, or a circle for a ring.
- **The built-in SRD's creature abilities.** With the built-in SRD on, every SRD creature's breath weapons, auras and bursts are listed in the cast window's Creature abilities, with no notes needed. Your own creature notes still come first.

## Fixes

- **The Set hotkeys button** now filters Obsidian's hotkeys list to Combat+'s commands (it filled in the search but didn't apply it).
- **In Atlas Online view,** spells set to show their animation only now get an Atlas area too, so online players still see them.
- **With animations for attacks on,** saves at one creature (Stunning Strike and the like) no longer draw a shape on the target; only attacks animate.
- **A spell's drawn colour** comes from its name and school, not from words its text uses in passing (like "dim light").

- **Every SRD creature ability now has a colour of its own** when drawn without JB2A: sleeping and slowing breaths, petrifying gazes, glows, darkness, stenches, fear auras, songs, dust and wing buffets no longer fall back to orange.
- **"Damage of the type chosen"** (a half-dragon's breath) is now rolled.

- **The introduction at the top of the settings** now shows as soon as they open, not only after you change something.
