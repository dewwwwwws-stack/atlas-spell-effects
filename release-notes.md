## What's new

- **Much less lag with lit spells and walls**: the add-on could end up redrawing the map thousands of times a second while a lit effect moved. It now draws once per screen refresh.
- **Cast window redesign**, in Atlas's own style: the spell list fills the left side, and the colour, opacity and buttons always stay in view at the bottom.
- **Drawn effects come first** in the animations, with a live preview of the shape in your colours, and an "Outer matches inner" option.
- **Cone angle**: set any cone's angle (53° for D&D 5e, 90° for Pathfinder…), with a default in the settings.
- **Drawn cones use both colours**: inner at the point, outer at the far end and edges.
- **Ending effects**: effects with the same name are numbered and say where they are and when they were cast; pointing at one outlines it on the map.
- **JB2A cones and rays reach their full length**: the empty space around each animation is measured once and trimmed away.
- **Tokens show through lasting effects** (players see only their own tokens through them).
- **Lighting**: darkness follows as you drag it; walls of light are lit by point lights along them (much lighter for Atlas); spell lights no longer show Atlas's light markers.
- Needs Obsidian 1.10 or later.
