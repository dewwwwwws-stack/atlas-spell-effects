## New

- **Healing is rolled.** Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Mass Cure Wounds, Prayer of Healing and others roll their healing, plus the caster's spellcasting modifier where the spell says so, with more for a higher slot. **Apply healing** raises each creature's hit points, never past their maximum.

## Fixes

- **Ice Knife is an attack again, then a save.** Its attack roll is made against the target (1d10 Piercing). Hit or miss, a second card rolls the Dexterity save for the target and every creature within 5 feet (2d6 Cold, more with a higher slot). The same goes for attacks with a damaging rider, like a giant spider's bite: its poison save is rolled on a hit only.
- **Attacks with a rider save stay attacks.** A bite that "must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition" is rolled as an attack, with **Give Prone** on its card, instead of as a save.
- **Attacks with more than one range use the farthest.** "Reach 5 ft. or range 150 ft." reaches 150 feet, not 5, so creatures further away show up in its menu.
- **Normal and long range.** A longbow (range 150/600) has disadvantage past 150 feet and is marked out of range past 600. An attack with one range (*range 150 ft.*) is marked out of range past it. A ranged attack at a creature next to the attacker has disadvantage, unless that creature can't act.
- **Distances are counted as D&D counts them.** A creature next to you is 5 ft away (it showed 0 ft before), diagonals count the same as straight lines, and "within 5 feet" no longer takes in a creature one square further.
- **Damage that comes later isn't added to the first hit.** Moonfire, Vitriolic Sphere, Melf's Acid Arrow, Enervation and others roll only what lands now, not what lands at the end of the target's next turn. Half on a save is read from "half the initial damage" too.
- **One damage type, not both, where you choose.** Spirit Guardians (Radiant *or* Necrotic) and Fire Shield (warm *or* chill) roll one type, not both added together.
- **Toll the Dead** rolls d12s instead of d8s on a creature missing any hit points.
- **Booming Blade and Green-Flame Blade** roll the weapon attack. Type your weapon's damage in the card's extra damage box; the 1d8 for moving isn't added on the hit.
- **Smites** (Divine, Searing, Thunderous…) deal their damage when cast, with no save of their own.
- **Hex, Hunter's Mark and Divine Favor** don't roll anything when cast. Add their dice to your hits with the extra damage box.
- **Higher slots grow the right damage.** Throw Glaive's Fire damage grows, not its Slashing. Wordings like "the damage increases by 1d8, for each slot level above 1st" (Catapult) are read now.
- **A type you choose** ("6d6 damage of the chosen type") is rolled, with no resistance applied.
