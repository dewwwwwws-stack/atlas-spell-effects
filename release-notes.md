## What's new

- **Creatures in spells are marked.** A creature inside a lasting spell gets Atlas's **In a spell** condition, plus the spell's own conditions (Silence: Deafened, Darkness: Blinded, Web: Restrained…), while it's inside. They come off when it leaves or the spell ends. Take a condition off a creature that saves and it stays off.
- **Instant spells to resolve.** Creatures caught in Fireball and the like are listed on a small card beside the map (yours only). Press **Failed** to give them the spell's conditions, or **Saved**.
- **Choose a spell's conditions** in the cast window's new **Conditions** row. They're read from the spell's text to start with.
- **Spell turn tracker.** During a fight, each lasting spell shows how many rounds it has been up (*Round 3*) beside it, on your screen only. Nothing ends on its own. Where the game system's round has a length (D&D and Pathfinder 6 seconds, Old-School Essentials and Cairn 10, Cyberpunk RED 3) it shows the time too (*Round 3 · 12 s*). Set or change it for any system with **Round length** in the settings.
- **Recent spells** at the top of ✨'s menu and the cast window.
- **Cast with no target** in a statblock's Cast menu plays the animation on the caster.
- **Saved spells keep their range** (so "Save as new…" spells show their range ring), and their sizes follow your game system's units if it changes.
