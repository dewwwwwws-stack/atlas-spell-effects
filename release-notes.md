# Combat+ 1.13.8

- **Spell notes from a template.** An `effect-…` property left blank, as in a note made from a spell template, now counts as not said, and Combat+ works it out from the spell's text. Before, a blank push, count, chain, teleport or conditions was read as "none", which could switch off what the text says the spell does.
- **Spells on a player's Spells tab castable again.** A statblock with tabs (Actions, Spells, Features…) keeps its other tabs hidden, and Combat+ only adds buttons to what's showing. It no longer noticed when you opened the Spells tab, so those spells got no Cast buttons. It now does.
- **Better wiring for a big bestiary.** Every action, trait and reaction in about 4,500 statblocks and every one of about 700 spells was checked:
  - *Multiattack:* far more are followed. That includes plain wording ("makes two melee attacks", "makes three melee attacks or two ranged attacks", "attacks twice, with its longsword or dagger", "uses Fire Ray twice"), weapons named loosely ("two longsword attacks" for a Frost Longsword, a Shortsword +2), "any combination of three Bite, Claw, or Spear attacks", and a summoned creature's "a number of attacks equal to half this spell's level" (up to four; press Esc when done). Of about 370 that weren't followed, about 20 are left, mostly Eye Rays, which aren't attacks.
  - *Summoned creatures* (spirits, familiars, companions): attacks written "Bonus equals your spell attack modifier" or "+3 plus PB to hit" now get an Attack button. The card asks for the bonus, and "+ the spell's level" or "plus PB" can be added on it.
  - *Damage dealt straight to a creature* ("…deals 9 (2d8) force damage to that creature") now gets a Use button that rolls it. Extra damage that rides on another attack is still left alone.
  - *An attack followed by a save in the same sentence* ("…piercing damage and the target must succeed on…") has its damage read again.
  - *Spell names with a slash:* "Enlarge/Reduce" or "Blindness/Deafness" in a statblock now matches your note "Enlarge-Reduce" (a note's name can't have a slash).
  - *More attack animations by name:* spines, thorns and barbs fly as darts; serrated and lancing bolts are crossbow bolts; orbs and jets fly as magic missiles; a few more bites and claws by name.
- **For Atlas Table Touch's combat panel.** Combat+ now gives other add-ons a creature's attacks and abilities (its Multiattack too), worked out just as its statblock buttons are, so players can attack from the player window as well as cast. Nothing changes in Combat+ itself.
