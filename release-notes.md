# Combat+ 1.13.6

- **Faster.** Combat+ was making Atlas sluggish on busy maps, worst with lasting spells up and tokens being dragged. It kept asking the browser to lay the whole page out again mid-frame, and rechecked who was standing in each spell on every frame. Now it remembers the map's size, checks spells at most five times a second while tokens move, and its drawing loop goes back to sleep sooner. In testing with 120 tokens and four lasting spells, its work while dragging went down to about a third, and while idle to about an eighth.
- **Big creatures' reach.** A Huge or Gargantuan creature's reach and range now count from the edge of its space, the way Atlas measures. Combat+ had read Atlas's token size as a number of squares, so a Gargantuan dragon was treated as 2½ squares across instead of 4. A Multiattack now picks the melee attack for a creature standing next to a big token's edge.
- **Animation style, in one place.** Settings → **Animation style** has three choices: *Atlas areas only* (just what Atlas Online players see), *Combat+ animations, with Atlas areas* (its own drawn animations, no JB2A), or *JB2A animations, with Atlas areas*. There are commands for each too.
- **Quicker casting from statblocks.** Clicking the top line of a statblock button's menu ("Attack: Multiattack", "Cast: Fireball") casts straight away, like clicking the button twice. **Shift+click** the button to skip the menu entirely.
