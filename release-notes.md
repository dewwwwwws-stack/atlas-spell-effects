## What's new

- **Bolts and rays hit their target.** Guiding Bolt, Scorching Ray, Eldritch Blast, Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, Magic Missile and the like are now laid out the way JB2A makes them (and Foundry's Sequencer plays them): the shot leaves the caster's middle and its impact lands on the target's middle, scaled evenly, so it's no longer squashed or stopping short.
- **The right length for the distance.** JB2A makes each bolt for 5, 15, 30, 60 and 90 ft; the one nearest the distance is used (fetched from JB2A the first time).
- **Cones stay cone-shaped.** An animation that isn't cone-shaped itself (fog, mist, darkness…) is now trimmed to the cone, with soft edges.
- **The cast window's list is A to Z**, and a long note ("bolts", "on creatures") no longer pushes a saved spell's name out of sight.
