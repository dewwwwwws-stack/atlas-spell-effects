# Combat+ 1.13.7

- **Faster again.** Combat+ was still making Atlas sluggish:
  - With any spell on the map, every movement of the mouse anywhere in Obsidian made it redraw, so it redrew constantly while you worked. Now only dragging something does.
  - Atlas updates its data all the time for things Combat+ doesn't use (fog, the player window, dice, its own panels). Combat+ was redrawing for every one of those; now it only redraws for changes to tokens, areas, lights, walls, the grid, selection and the like.
  - It was rechecking statblocks for buttons whenever anything on the page changed. Now it only does so when a statblock changes.
  - Pointing at the map no longer makes the browser work out the whole page layout on every mouse move, and the hit cards remember where the map is.
- **Breath weapons and other big areas drawn at their full size.** With *Combat+ animations*, an instant area's burst could shrink to a small wedge in the middle of the area, most noticeably on a big cone (a dragon's 90 ft breath) on a map with large grid squares. The burst's grow-in replaced the scaling that fits the picture to the area instead of adding to it. It's now drawn at its full size.
- **Monster abilities matched up for all three animation styles.** Every area ability in the SRD (2014 and 2024) and the bestiary was checked, about 2,500 of them, for its JB2A animation, Combat+'s own colours, and Atlas's area look:
  - *JB2A:* Wing Attack makes a gust of air instead of a volley of arrows. Death Burst and Death Throes explode in their own element (frost, poison, lightning, necrotic…) instead of always fire. A one-off burst round a creature plays as a burst, while auras (Fire Aura, Cold Aura) keep their ring. Auras that deal a set amount of damage ("takes 10 necrotic damage") now get their element's animation.
  - *Combat+ colours:* a Darkness Aura is dark, not lamp-yellow. Antimagic, time, wind, plants, blood and rallying auras get their own colours instead of the default.
  - *Atlas looks (what Atlas areas only shows):* poison, sleep, slowing, weakening, paralysing and repulsion breaths, stenches and spores get Mist. Illumination gets Radiance. Something that only mentions fire without dealing it (a Harpy's Luring Song) gets no Fire look.
  - *Attacks:* a Purple Worm's or Kraken's Bite is now an attack again. It had been read as an area because of what it says about a creature "within 10 feet" after being swallowed.
- **Choosing a Multiattack's attack.** Where a Multiattack can use more than one attack, buttons appear under the hint: *By distance*, then each attack. Click one, press its number key (**0** for by distance), or press **Tab** to step through them. While it's going by distance, the first button shows which attack the creature under the pointer would get.
