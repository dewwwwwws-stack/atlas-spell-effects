## What's new

- **Spells sit behind the tokens again.** Every token, whatever its size, stands on top of every spell, with nothing cut out of the spell around it. That also fixes the players seeing where a token was standing in darkness: a token the players can't see (hidden, or in darkness) leaves no trace.
- Spells are lit by Atlas's scene lighting, like the floor, as before.
- Effects on a creature (Shield, Bless) sit just over their token, and only show to the players when they can see that creature.
