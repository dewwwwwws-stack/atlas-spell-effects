## New

- **Apply all.** When two or more cards have damage waiting (a Multiattack, or a whole turn's attacks), an **Apply all** button at the top of the cards applies every one at once, with each creature's total shown. It has a hotkey command too: *Apply damage from every card (the whole turn)*.
- **Casting from Atlas's DM screen.** A statblock's Cast, Use or Attack button on the DM screen knows its creature, puts the DM screen away so you can see the map, and brings it back once you've finished aiming and applied the damage.
- **The Cast, Use or Attack menu now says what it's for** at the top (for example *Cast: Eldritch Blast*, then *By Archmage*), so you can tell even if the statblock has closed.

## Fixes

- **"In a spell" no longer gets stuck.** Only lasting spells (Darkness, Spirit Guardians…) put it on creatures. Instant spells and attacks no longer do, and any left over from before are taken off.
- **A bolt cast from the cast window** (Eldritch Blast, Fire Bolt) now rolls as the caster you click first: their attack bonus, and their level for extra beams. Before, it rolled with no caster.
- **A roll that fails now says so.** If a cast can't be rolled, a message says why instead of nothing happening. If a player character's cast isn't rolled because rolling is set to NPCs only, you're told once.
- **Player character sheets in Atlas:** a save attack written as "Failed save: 2d6 Psychic" now rolls its damage, and the sheet's Defences count as resistances.
- **Built-in SRD creature notes** now list saving throws by the ability's full name (dexterity, not dex), so Atlas's own 5E statblock shows them. Notes already written keep the old names until you write them again from the SRD.
