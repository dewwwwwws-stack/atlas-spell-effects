## New

- **Multiattacks with a choice** ("three attacks, using Greatsword or Longbow in any combination"): each attack is picked by how far the target is. Within reach it's the melee one, further off the ranged one. Press **Tab** while aiming to choose the next one yourself; the hint says which attack is next. With animations for attacks on, each attack plays its own weapon's animation.
- **Bonuses to spell damage, added for you:** Agonizing Blast (Charisma to Eldritch Blast), Empowered Evocation (Intelligence to Evocation spells), Elemental Affinity (Charisma to spells of the dragon's damage type) and Potent Spellcasting (Wisdom to cantrips), when the caster's statblock names them. The card shows each one, for example *8d6 fire + 4 (Empowered Evocation)*.
- **Genie's Wrath:** a Genie warlock's attack cards get a button for it (proficiency bonus, in the patron's damage type).

## Fixes

- **Attack buttons now show with only animations for attacks on.** Before, statblock attacks only got buttons with rolling on.
