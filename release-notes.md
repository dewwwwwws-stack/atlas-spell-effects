## Fixes

- **Casting from a token's statblock knows the caster again.** A statblock window opened from a token no longer says "No caster on the map" when you press Cast a while after opening it. "Edit statblock" from a token's menu finds its token too.
- **The caster isn't caught in their own spell.** Lightning Bolt, Burning Hands and other lines, cones and auras from a caster no longer list or mark the caster.
- **No Cast button on the "to resolve" card.** The card's spell name was being mistaken for a statblock.
