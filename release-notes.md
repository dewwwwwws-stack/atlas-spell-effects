## What's new

- **Cast buttons in statblocks.** Every spell the add-on knows in an Atlas statblock now has a small **Cast** button in front of its name, instead of being underlined. It opens a menu showing who's casting and the spell's range, how it's cast (*Click where it goes on the map*, *Aim it on the map*, *Click a token on the map*, *Cast on Archmage*…), and *Open in the cast window…* to change it first.
- **Fewer spells missed, in player statblocks especially.** Spells are now also found under any heading, label or tab about spells (*Spells*, *Cantrips*, *Prepared*…), as chips or entries of their own, in a feat or feature that lists them (*Magic Initiate: Fire Bolt, Mage Hand, Shield*), and, for names of two words or more (*Misty Step*), anywhere in a statblock that has spells. Things under *Equipment*, *Inventory* or *Magic Items* are left alone, so a shield stays a shield.
