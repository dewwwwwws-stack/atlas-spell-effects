## What's new

- **Saved spells: one thing instead of presets and defaults.** Set a spell up as you like it, then:
  - **Save**: that spell is cast like this from now on (written onto its own note);
  - **Save as new…**: a second version under its own name ("Big blue Fireball");
  - **In ✨ menu**: cast it in one click from the map's ✨ button;
  - **Delete** a spell of your own, or **Forget saved** on one of your spell pages (its note is kept, only the effect settings come off).

  Your presets become saved spells automatically, still in ✨'s menu.
- **Players see where you aim** (optional, in ✨'s menu or the settings): while you place a spell, the players' screen shows its area, so they can see what it will cover before you cast.
- **Spells stay visible in the dark.** They're drawn above Atlas's scene lighting, so Darkness, and any spell in a dim room, shows at full strength. Tokens, their bars and handles stay on top.
- **Magical darkness blocks sight.** Darkness puts up a ring of sight-blocking Atlas walls round its edge: no one sees into, out of or through it, and light doesn't pass through. Tokens can still walk in. The walls move with it and go when it ends.
- **A step-by-step guide to setting up spell Bases** in the README.
- **Range when casting from a statblock.** A spell cast at a point (Fireball) shows its range as a dashed ring round the caster and can't be placed beyond it. A cone or line (Lightning Bolt) always starts from the caster. The caster is found from the statblock: the token it was opened from, the token selected on the map, or the one token linked to a statblock note open in a tab. If it can't tell, it asks you to click the caster first.
- **Choose what the players see while you aim:** an outline (the default), filled, or the spell's own animation.
- **Every game system built into Atlas.** Units, cone angle and range bands come from the map's collection in Atlas. Sizes and ranges are in your game's own unit, as Atlas's lights and rulers are: feet, yards (Call of Cthulhu), metres (Cyberpunk RED) or squares (Draw Steel). Spell text in each game's wording is read too: "20-foot burst", "6 m radius", "20' radius", "10 yards", "3 burst", "within Very Close range", "a Near-sized cube". Size steps are one square. The cone angle setting is now only an override.
- **A statblock template with spells** (`examples/` on GitHub), and a guide to where to get statblocks with spells.
- **Every spell in a statblock can be cast**, including ones with no area (Shield lands on its caster; Mage Hand on a square in range). Spells are found in 2024 Spellcasting actions, Pathfinder's spell lines, Fantasy Statblocks' layout, Atlas's spell tabs, actions named after a spell, and links to spell notes, and by their other names (a note's aliases, "Hideous Laughter" for "Tasha's Hideous Laughter").
