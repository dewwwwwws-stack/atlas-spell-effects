## Fixes

- **The × on the "hit" card really closes it now.** After an update, the old copy of the add-on left its card behind with a × that did nothing, sitting under the new one. Old cards are cleared, and × also answers a plain click.
- **Cones match Atlas's.** The cone you aim is now the shape Atlas draws: your game's cone angle, rounded at the end (or flat, if your game's rules say so). Circles, squares and lines already matched.
- The same spell can no longer show up on the "hit" card twice.
- Tidied the code flagged by Obsidian's plugin review (no change to how anything works).
- Releases now come with GitHub's build attestations, so anyone can check `main.js` and `styles.css` were built from this repo.
- The README now says plainly what the add-on looks through in your vault, and why.
