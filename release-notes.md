## New

- **Pick a target from the Cast menu.** Spells cast at creatures (Fire Bolt, Hold Person…) now list every creature in range, nearest first, with the distance. Pick one and it's cast straight away.
- **Spell Effects inside Atlas's own area window.** Click an area's handle: a spell's area has **Edit spell…** and **End spell**; one of your own areas has **Animate…**. This replaces ✨ → *Animate one of Atlas's areas…*.

## Fixes

- **The × on the "hit" card closes it now.** Atlas's map was swallowing the click.
- **Cone of Cold (and other spells with a look) show in the look's colour.** Frost is pale blue, not the spell's orange. A colour you set in Atlas is still kept.
- Settings show up in Obsidian's settings search (Obsidian 1.13 and later).
- Tidied the code and styles flagged by Obsidian's plugin review.
