# Combat+ 1.13.12

- **Give any ability a Use button.** An ability whose text names nothing Combat+ can read had no button and no way to get one. Now right-click its name in the statblock and pick **Give … a Use button**. The cast window opens: choose its shape, size, range and look, then **Save its button**. Every statblock with an ability of that name then has a **Use** button for it. Right-click its name again to change it or take the button away.
- **Daggerheart features read better.** Besides "all targets within Close range" (an area round the adversary), Combat+ now reads:
  - "in front of it within Close range" as a cone;
  - "in a line within Far range" as a line;
  - "a point within Far range … all targets within Very Close range of that point" as a circle placed up to Far away;
  - "make an attack against a target within Close range" as cast on the creature you click, within that range;
  - a *Passive* feature with an area as an aura that stays on the map.
- **Every game system Atlas has, read better.** A sweep of how each system's statblocks word their areas:
  - *Pathfinder 2e*: auras ("(aura, olfactory) 30 feet", always on) and a burst's range ("10-foot burst within 60 feet");
  - *Old-School Essentials*: "Cone 90' long", "Line 100' long, 5' wide", "Cloud 50' × 40' × 20'" and "All within 30'";
  - *Cairn*: "in a cone" with no size (three squares) and "all adjacent targets";
  - *Call of Cthulhu*: "everyone within 3 yards" and "a gas cloud with a radius of 5 yards";
  - *Cyberpunk RED*: "a 3m wide cone, 8m long" and "a 10m/yds x 10m/yds square area";
  - *Shadowdark*: "One target within far range", "creatures within close range of it", "a line, near length";
  - *Draw Steel*: "2 aura" (always on), "10 wall within 10", and strikes ("Distance Melee 1 / Ranged 10, Target One creature"; "Melee 1 or ranged 5" reaches 5), cast on the creature you click.
  - in any game: "anyone meeting its gaze within 30'" is round the creature, and "out to a radius of 30 feet" round itself (or round the point it names).
  In D&D, a few monsters' abilities read better too: wails and howls on "each creature within 30 feet" are now an area round the creature, "a 5-foot-wide line that is 60 feet long" a line, and "pollen out to a radius of 30 feet" a circle round it. Storm of Vengeance is now its 300-foot circle.
