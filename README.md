# Spell Effects for AtlasVTT

An Obsidian plugin that adds animated spells and abilities on top of Atlas's own spell areas. Spells are cast as real Atlas areas, so players on Atlas Online see the area, its look and its light. The JB2A animations play in your Obsidian only, and the ability to cast spells is purely from the DM side within statblocks in Obsidian.

## Animations
- JB2A's free animations play over the area: Fireball's blast, Fire Breath's cone, Spirit Guardians around the cleric
- Bolts like Fire Bolt fly from the caster to the target
- A fitting animation is picked automatically, by name and damage type, for every game system Atlas supports
- Animate any area you drew yourself with Atlas's Measure tool
- Areas with no animation can be still, breathing, pulsing, shimmering or flickering
- Download the JB2A Free Library in the plugins settings
- ***DM-Side Only, Custom Animations will not be visible in Atlas Online***

## Cast straight from statblocks
- A **Cast** button next to every spell in a creature's or player's statblock
- A **Use** button on abilities with an area: Fire Breath, Antimagic Cone, auras and so on, read from the statblock's own text
- Press the button twice to cast straight away, or hover over it to preview how it'll look
- Spells cast at a creature list everyone in range, nearest first. Pick one and it's cast
- ***DM-Side Only, players are not able to cast spells from Atlas Online***

## Your own spell library
- Spells come from your own spell notes and Bases
- Save a spell's animation, colour, look and area once and it's used every time
- Save an ability's look once and every statblock with that ability uses it

## What spells actually do on the map
- **Pushes and pulls:** Thunderwave, Thorn Whip and the like move the creatures they catch
- **Teleports:** Misty Step and Dimension Door move the caster's token
- **Counted bolts:** Magic Missile's darts, Scorching Ray's rays; Eldritch Blast's beams go up with the caster's level
- **Chain spells:** Chain Lightning leaps on to the nearest creatures
- **Several areas:** Meteor Swarm's four blasts, placed one after another
- **Summoned things:** Spiritual Weapon and Flaming Sphere get a token of their own to drag around
- **Difficult terrain:** Spike Growth, Web and Entangle count towards Atlas's movement ruler while they last

## Keeping track
- A card names who an instant spell hit
- Lasting spells put conditions on those inside (Blinded in Darkness, for instance)
- Shows how many rounds each lasting spell has been up, from Atlas's initiative
- Walls drawn corner by corner, and rings (Wall of Fire, Wall of Force)
- An option for every area to go through walls

## Inside Atlas
- Click a spell's area in Atlas to **Edit** or **End** it from Atlas's own window
- Cones and circles match Atlas's own shapes exactly
